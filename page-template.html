Know what you’re starting

A guide for your chat.

The prompt asks your chat to use a six-direction compass, keep track of the route within its available context, distinguish evidence from uncertainty, and write either Spiral Course style when requested.

It does not add permanent memory, web access, voice generation, or a live 3D interface. Use save for a portable checkpoint. Research, images, audio, HTML, and publishing depend on tools and access you actually have.

No signup or installation on this page. Your chosen AI service may require an account and apply its own limits. The copy button uses your clipboard; this page does not send a topic or prompt to an AI service.
